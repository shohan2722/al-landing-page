FAQ

Questions We're Happy to Answer

On Results

Will I look natural, or will people be able to tell I've had work done? +
Natural results are not just our goal — they are our standard. Our providers take a conservative, layered approach to every injectable treatment. We focus on subtle refinement: softening lines without eliminating expression, restoring volume without changing proportion, enhancing features without erasing character. Our clients routinely tell us the most common comment they receive is “you look incredible — did you change your hair?” That's exactly what we're aiming for.
How quickly will I see results? +
This depends on the treatment. Botox and Dysport typically show results within 7–14 days. Dermal fillers provide immediate volume with final results visible once any minor swelling subsides. Facials, peels, and laser treatments often show progressive improvement over days to weeks. Sculptra and PRP deliver results that build over months. During your consultation, we'll give you a realistic timeline specific to your treatment plan.
What if I'm not happy with my results? +
We set honest, realistic expectations during your consultation — and we follow up after every treatment. If something doesn't look the way we planned, we will work with you directly to address it. Our conservative approach is designed specifically to reduce the risk of outcomes that need correction.

On Treatments

I don't know which treatments I need. Where do I start? +
This is the most common thing our new clients tell us — and it's exactly why the consultation exists. Come in, share your goals, and let our providers guide you. You don't need to arrive with a treatment in mind. We'll build the plan together.
Do the injectable treatments hurt? +
Most clients describe injectable treatments as mildly uncomfortable — not painful. We use fine needles, topical numbing where appropriate, and techniques designed to minimize discomfort. Most clients are surprised by how manageable the experience is.
Is there downtime after my treatments? +
This varies by treatment type. Botox and Dysport have virtually no downtime — most clients return to normal activity the same day. Fillers may cause minor swelling or bruising for a few days. Laser treatments and deeper peels may require 3–7 days of recovery depending on the type and depth. We'll discuss downtime in detail during your consultation so you can plan accordingly.
